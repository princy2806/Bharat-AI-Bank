- Read at 130-140 words per minute
- Pause for 2-3 seconds after major statements

**EMPHASIS:**

## HINDI PHRASE PRONUNCIATION

If you need to say the Hindi:

1. **नमस्ते** (Namaste)
   - Pronunciation: "nuh-MUS-tay"
   - Meaning: Hello/Greeting

2. **विवाद** (Vivad - Dispute)
   - Pronunciation: "vi-VAHD"
   - Meaning: Dispute/Disagreement

3. **रसीद** (Raseed - Receipt)
   - Pronunciation: "ruh-SEED"
   - Meaning: Receipt

4. **कृपया अपनी रसीद अपलोड करें** 
   - Pronunciation: "kri-PAY-uh uh-nee ruh-SEED uh-plohd kuh-REN"
   - Meaning: "Please upload your receipt"

**NOTE:** If Hindi pronunciation is difficult, you can just speak the English translation instead. The meaning will be clear to viewers.
