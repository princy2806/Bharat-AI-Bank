🎯 PROJECT OVERVIEW

Bharat AI Bank is an AI-powered banking solution built on Salesforce Agentforce that delivers:

✅ Credit Card Dispute Resolution in 90 seconds (vs 7-10 days)
✅ Intelligent Document Processing with Document AI/IDP
✅ Multilingual Support (English & Hindi)
✅ Real-time Account Balance & Statement Access
✅ Autonomous EMI Restructuring & Callback Scheduling
✅ Zero Manual Processing - Fully Automated

📽️ IN THIS DEMO VIDEO

**Scenario 1: Account Balance & Statement Check (2-3 min)**
- Customer asks for account balance in Hindi
- Agent responds with real-time data: ₹97,650
- Generates and emails 30-day statement PDF instantly

**Scenario 2: Credit Card Dispute (3-4 min)**
- Fraudulent transaction detected: ₹5,999
- Customer uploads credit card statement
- Document AI instantly extracts transaction details
- Salesforce Case created automatically (Case #DIS-2025-00845)
- Real-time tracking provided with SMS & email confirmation

**Scenario 3: EMI Restructuring (4-5 min)**
- Family emergency: Need to reduce home loan EMI
- Agent shows 3 restructuring options
- Option A selected: EMI reduced from ₹25,000 to ₹18,500
- Relationship Manager callback scheduled for next day (10 AM - 12 PM)
- All confirmations sent via email & SMS

❓ FAQ

Q: Is this production-ready?
A: This is a hackathon proof-of-concept. Production deployment would require additional security hardening, compliance certifications, and scaled infrastructure.

Q: What languages are supported?
A: Currently English and Hindi. Can be extended to other Indian regional languages (Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, etc.)

Q: How fast is Document AI processing?
A: Typically 2-3 seconds per document, including data extraction and Salesforce case creation.

Q: Can this handle complex disputes?
A: Simple disputes are fully automated. Complex disputes are escalated to human agents via Omnichannel routing.

Q: What's the cost of implementation?
A: Depends on org scale and volume. See GitHub README for cost estimates.
