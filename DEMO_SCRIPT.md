### [SCENE 1: CUSTOMER PROBLEM - 1 minute]

**Let me introduce Rahul, a typical bank customer who just noticed something concerning on his credit card statement.**

**Rahul sees a fraudulent transaction of Rs 4,500 that he doesn't recognize. In traditional banking:**
- He calls the bank helpline
- Waits 25 minutes on hold
- The IVR system doesn't understand his Hindi request
- He gets transferred three times between departments
- Finally, someone asks him to visit the nearest branch with physical documents
- He fills out a paper form and is told: "Resolution takes 7-10 business days"
- He has zero visibility into the status of his dispute
- Meanwhile, his credit limit is blocked
- **Frustrated, he considers switching banks**

---

### [SCENE 2: SOLUTION WITH BHARAT AI BANK - 2 minutes 30 seconds]

**Now, let's see the same scenario with Bharat AI Bank:**

**[SHOW SCREEN: Experience Cloud Chat Interface]**

**Rahul opens the Bharat AI Bank app on his phone and starts a chat:**

**Rahul:** "Namaste! I want to dispute a transaction of Rs 4,500 on my credit card."

**[SHOW: AI Agent responds in Hindi]**

**Agentforce AI Agent:** "नमस्ते! आपके विवाद को संभालने में मदद करके खुश हूँ। कृपया अपनी रसीद या बिल अपलोड करें।" 
*(Hello! I'm happy to help with your dispute. Please upload your receipt or bill.)*

**[SHOW SCREEN: File Upload]**

**Rahul uploads his credit card statement PDF showing the fraudulent transaction.**

**[HIGHLIGHT: Behind the scenes - Document AI Processing]**

**Here's where the magic happens — our Document AI (IDP) technology instantly:**
- Reads the PDF
- Extracts the transaction amount (Rs 4,500)
- Identifies the merchant name and date
- Populates all required fields automatically
- **All in under 2 seconds!**

**[SHOW SCREEN: Case created in Salesforce]**

**Instantly, a Salesforce Case is created with:**
- Transaction details automatically populated
- Priority set to HIGH (fraud case)
- Assigned to the fraud investigation team
- Full audit trail logged

**[SHOW: Agent responds]**

**Agentforce AI Agent:** "आपके विवाद की शिकायत सफलतापूर्वक दर्ज की गई है! केस नंबर: #12345। आप अपने दावे को यहाँ ट्रैक कर सकते हैं।"
*(Your dispute has been successfully filed! Case number: #12345. You can track your claim here.)*

**[SHOW SCREEN: Real-time status updates]**

Rahul receives:
- Instant case confirmation number
- Real-time status updates in the chat
- SMS and email notifications
- Estimated resolution timeline

**[SCREEN: Balance check feature]**

**Bonus — Rahul also asks: "मेरा current balance क्या है?" (What's my current balance?)**

**The agent immediately responds with:**
- Current account balance: Rs 97,650
- Available credit limit: Rs 2,15,000
- Outstanding due: Rs 45,000
- Payment due date: May 15, 2025

**All live data from the core banking system — no manual lookup required!**

## TIPS FOR RECORDING

1. **Speak clearly** — Pronunciation is important
2. **Pace yourself** — 130-140 words per minute is comfortable
3. **Show screen transitions** — Wait 2-3 seconds on each screen
4. **Highlight key moments** — When Document AI extracts data, pause
5. **Use sound effects** — Notification "ding" when case is created
6. **Include subtitles** — For accessibility
7. **Background music** — Soft, upbeat, professional tone
8. **Lighting** — Well-lit face, clear camera
