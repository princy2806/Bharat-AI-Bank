## SPEAKING TIPS

**Pacing:**
- Speak at 130-140 words per minute
- Pause between sentences (2 seconds)
- Let videos play without talking during transitions

**Tone:**
- Professional but friendly
- Conversational, not robotic
- Show enthusiasm about the solution

**Key Moments (Pause Here):**
1. When showing the PDF upload (let people see the file)
2. When Document AI extracts data (dramatic pause for effect)
3. When Case is created in Salesforce (show the case creation)
4. When balance is shown (let people read the numbers)

**Hindi Pronunciation** (if needed):

- Agentforce = uh-JEN-tee-forse

## RECORDING CHECKLIST

Before you record:
- [ ] Quiet room, no background noise
- [ ] Good lighting on your face
- [ ] Clear, quality microphone
- [ ] Camera steady (tripod recommended)
- [ ] Have your script printed or on second screen
- [ ] Warm up your voice
- [ ] Do a test recording first

During recording:
- [ ] Speak clearly and naturally
- [ ] Make eye contact with camera
- [ ] Use hand gestures (authentic, not forced)
- [ ] Smile when appropriate (especially at opening)
- [ ] Pause between major points
- [ ] Don't rush — let ideas land

After recording:
- [ ] Review for sound quality
- [ ] Check for any "ums" or "ahs"
- [ ] Trim silences at start/end
- [ ] Add subtitles for accessibility
- [ ] Add soft background music if desired
